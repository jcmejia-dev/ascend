# Catalog sources

Checked October 11, 2026. Difficulty and career relevance are ASCEND editorial guidance. Provider-published durations are identified separately from adjustable personal planning estimates. Unknown fields say “Not specified.” Resource hubs and documentation are labeled and do not imply a fixed course syllabus. Current requirements must be checked on the provider's site before enrollment.

- freeCodeCamp: [Learning catalog](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/); [provider announcement of current certifications](https://www.freecodecamp.org/news/freecodecamps-new-python-certification-is-now-live). Learning and certifications are free; provider requirements apply.
- Harvard CS50: [Certificate requirements](https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/certificate/), [FAQs](https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/faqs/). A free CS50 certificate differs from the paid edX verified option; assignments and final project requirements apply.
- Full Stack Open: [General information](https://fullstackopen.com/en/part0/general_info/). Free study and course certificate with qualifying exercise completion.
- IBM SkillsBuild: [Provider homepage](https://skillsbuild.org/) and [digital credentials](https://skillsbuild.org/digital-credentials). Free learning, with credentials depending on the chosen path.
- TESDA Online Program: [Portal](https://e-tesda.gov.ph/), [TESDA's explanation of free courses, completion certificates, and separate national assessment](https://tesda.gov.ph/Media/NewsDetail/20051). A TOP completion certificate does not itself grant a National Certificate.
- The Odin Project: [FAQ](https://www.theodinproject.com/faq). Free curriculum; no completion certificate.
- MDN Web Docs: [Curriculum](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/curriculum/) and [learning materials](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Learn_web_development). Free materials; no completion certificate advertised.
- Microsoft Learn: [Training](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/), [Microsoft Learn overview](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-learn), [credential overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/). Free self-directed training; learning achievements and exam/lab-based credentials differ. Exam costs depend on the credential.
- Official Laravel: [Installation and documentation index](https://laravel.com/docs/installation). A free framework reference; not a provider-certified course. Requires PHP and local setup. No fixed duration is published on the referenced overview.
- Official React: [Quick Start](https://react.dev/learn). Components, rendering data, events, state and sharing data are listed in the official page. A free documentation resource, with no completion credential or fixed duration advertised there.

The current CS50 overview identifies eleven weeks of material, and its certificate page requires at least 70% on each problem set and the final project for a free CS50 certificate. Full Stack Open's general information identifies fourteen parts and a flexible average of 15–20 hours per part. These are provider statements; ASCEND's week estimates use separate planning-hour values divided by the user's weekly schedule.

The freeCodeCamp pages are dynamic and did not expose readable curriculum content during this check. The resource preview explicitly reports limited metadata verification and leaves curriculum, language and duration unspecified. It does not invent a current module count or certificate threshold. The original eight-entry tracker catalog remains available; the dedicated Discover Courses layer adds these details and the two official documentation resources.

Content and security reference sources: [CodeMirror basic setup](https://codemirror.net/examples/basic/), [MDN iframe sandbox](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Elements/iframe), [Ollama chat API](https://github.com/ollama/ollama/blob/main/docs/api.md), and [MCP authorization specification](https://github.com/modelcontextprotocol/modelcontextprotocol/blob/main/docs/specification/2025-06-18/basic/authorization.mdx). ASCEND's lessons and questions are original; none are copied from those providers' curricula.
